# FELVERIS

**Open source. Privacy. Security. Community.**

FELVERIS is an independent, community-oriented initiative with an
interest in open source, privacy, and security.

It is currently run independently by its founder as a personal project.
Its direction and projects may grow over time, with room for others to
get involved.

## What matters to us

-   **Open source** --- supporting software and resources that can be
    inspected, shared, and improved.
-   **Privacy** --- respecting people and their personal data.
-   **Security** --- taking a thoughtful, responsible approach to
    protecting people and systems.
-   **Community** --- valuing openness, collaboration, and learning from
    one another.

## Explore

Visit the repositories in this organization to see its current work and
resources.

## Get involved

Ideas, feedback, and constructive discussion are welcome. If you are
interested in contributing to a project, check its repository for
contribution guidelines. Where no guidelines exist yet, open an issue to
